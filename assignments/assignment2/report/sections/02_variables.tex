\subsection{Conventions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Bold lowercase symbols are 3-vectors; lengths in mm, angles in rad. $F=\Frame(R,\vct{p})$, $F\vct{x}=R\vct{x}+\vct{p}$, $\sk(\vct{u})\vct{v}=\vct{u}\times\vct{v}$; small errors $\Delta F=\Frame(I+\sk(\vct{\alpha}),\vct{\epsilon})$.
\item Frames: workspace, tracker $\{\mathrm{trk}\}$, robot base $\{\mathrm{rob}\}$, stage output $\{c\}$, wrist center $\{w\}$, end-effector $\{e\}$. $F_{x,y}$ is the pose of $y$ relative to $x$; single-subscript poses keep the handout's meaning.
\item Unmarked: nominal. $^*$: actual (hidden). Leading subscript $\mathrm{obs}$: tracker output; $\mathrm{est}$: our calibration result; $\mathrm{bl}$, $\mathrm{init}$: baseline and initial estimates. $j$ indexes calibration samples, $k$ joints or markers.
\item $mBee$ coincides with $\{e\}$, so its pose relative to the base is $F_{\mathrm{ee}}$ in every version (the handout's $F_{mBee}$); the subscript $\mathrm{mBee}$ is used only relative to the tracker. The tool-mounting error $\Delta F_{\mathrm{ee}}(\vct{\eta}_{\mathrm{ee}})$ of the handout is written $\Delta F_{\mathrm{we}}$.
\end{itemize}
